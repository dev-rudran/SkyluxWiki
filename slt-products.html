Travel Care Service

v7.0 · v4.0

Travel Care Service (TCS) is an add-on product customers purchase alongside their airline tickets to protect their journey from unexpected disruptions and make their ticket more flexible. It is offered in 3 plans: Basic, Premium, and All-Included. TCS v7.0 is designed specifically for upgrade ticket cases, while TCS v4.0 serves standard bookings. Both versions follow the same core benefit structure.

Core Benefits

Price Drop Protection

Monitors the fare for 24 hours after booking. If the price drops, Skylux issues a credit voucher for the difference (max $1,000). Only the highest drop is credited.

All Plans

Flight Delay & Cancellation Coverage

Complimentary rebooking if the flight is disrupted on the day of travel. Covers up to $1,000/passenger. Includes missed connection guarantee and service satisfaction guarantee.

All Plans

$300 Service Satisfaction Guarantee

If arrival is delayed 6+ hours and the customer is unhappy with rebooking options, a $300 SLT credit voucher is issued. (v4.0: up to $500).

All Plans

Agency Service Fees Waived

All Skylux agency service fees are waived when changes are needed. Travelers only pay airline penalties or fare differences.

Premium +

Lost Baggage Protection

$2,000 per bag (up to 2 bags) if luggage is not returned within 96 hours. No proof of contents required.

All-Included

$150 Unused Benefit Voucher

If no TCS benefits are used during the trip, customers receive a $150 SLT credit voucher. Valid 1 year, transferable.

TCS v7.0 vs v4.0 Key Differences

  • ▸ v7.0 is designed specifically for upgrade ticket cases. It appears automatically when an upgrade is identified.
  • ▸ In GBO Sale Wizard, check the upgrade box then refresh the GBO container to reveal the "Upgrade Fee" column (default $0).
  • ▸ Upgrade cases marked with the flag will not go into auto ticketing — they require manual processing.
  • ▸ v4.0 is for standard (non-upgrade) bookings and includes Cancel for Any Reason + $150 voucher in the All-Included plan.
  • ▸ v4.0 Service Satisfaction Guarantee is up to $500; v7.0 offers a flat $300 credit voucher.

Skylux works with the airline to locate and return the bags, and if not found within 96 hours, pays a guaranteed $2,000 per bag (up to 2 bags) — no proof of contents required.

Exchange / Cancel for Any Reason

Standalone

Exchange / Cancel for Any Reason bundles two standalone add-on products that give customers flexibility when plans change — Change for Any Reason (voluntary exchange) and Cancel for Any Reason (voluntary cancellation). These are designed for customers who booked a flight ticket and did not choose a TCS bundle.

Change for Any Reason

One Free Change

Customers can make one qualified change to their booking for any reason without facing airline penalties or agency processing fees.

Flexible Segment Changes

Before the first flight: change any or all segments. After the trip starts: change remaining segments only; the unused portion cost is covered.

What Can Be Changed

Date, time, and one-way/round-trip nature of the booking. The new itinerary must depart within 1 year from the original purchase.

What Is Covered

Airline penalties and agency processing fees are covered. Customers only pay any fare difference if the new ticket costs more.

Pricing

Price: 13% of ticket value · min $42.95 (whichever is higher)

Rules & Limitations

  • ▸ Cannot be used to cancel and receive a full or partial refund — exchange only
  • ▸ New itinerary must depart within 1 year from original ticket purchase date
  • ▸ Only one qualified change permitted per product purchase

Cancel for Any Reason

If the customer holds a Cancel for Any Reason plan, they can cancel their flight for any reason prior to the first scheduled departure and receive either a refund or credit.

Two Settlement Options

Choose between 80% cash refund of the ticket cost, or 100% airline credit for future travel with the same airline.

24-Hour Cutoff

Cancellation must be made at least 24 hours before the first scheduled departure.

Skylux-Only Cancellation

All cancellations must be processed through Skylux, not the airline directly. No reason needs to be provided.

Cash vs Credit Rules

Cash refund only applicable for fully unused tickets. Airline credit is valid for 1 year from original issue date; cannot be transferred or combined.

Priority Disruption Assistance

Standalone

Priority Disruption Assistance (PDA) is an add-on service that allows customers to rebook an alternative flight if their flight is delayed by 2+ hours, canceled, or if they miss a connecting flight due to a prior delay or cancellation. It provides a complimentary rebooking on the day of travel when the airline does not offer a reasonable alternative.

  • ▸ Disruption must occur while in transit on the day of the originally scheduled flight
