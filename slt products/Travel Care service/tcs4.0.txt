Hi and welcome to Travel Care Service version 4 product overview. Welcome to the next level of travel
assistance. Our newly updated Travel Care service is set to redefine the travel experience for our customers.
Travel Care Service 4.0 makes air travel more flexible and protected regardless of the airline or type of ticket
being booked. A suite of services offers Skylux travelers a new way to fly with more flexibility and protection
than ever. The service ensures that travelers get to their destination in case of flight disruptions, allows them
to make changes to their trip, and offers a great possibility with cancellation for any reason option. As travel
consultant, you play a crucial role in introducing our customers to these enhanced offerings. In this
presentation, we will delve into the key features, advantages, and how to effectively present the Travel care
service to our valued clients. Let's embark on this journey of providing peace of mind for every travel
adventure. Airfare is usually the most expensive and least flexible piece of a trip, so it's important to protect
the most valuable part of traveler's next journey. Every airline has complex rules for the different types of
tickets they sell. So when things go wrong, it can be maddening and costly to fix it all. That's why we decided
to make it incredibly simple and create one suite of benefits to address all the risks so that our clients can
focus on having the best trip possible. Our goal with Travel Care Services 4.0 is to give our travelers the
ultimate peace of mind. As you already know, Travel Care service is a product that your customers can
purchase in addition to their airline tickets in order to protect their trip from the most common unpleasant
scenarios and make tickets less restrictive. Our Travelcare service is presented in 3 plans basic, premium,
and all included. Let's explore these tiers and the benefits they offer. The basic tier is traveller's foundation
for a seamless travel experience. It encompasses price drop protection, flight delay, and cancellation, and
missed connection, elevating customers' travel experience. The premium tier includes all the benefits of the
basic tier, along with change for any reason option, waived agency service fees, and making voluntary
changes hassle free. The pinnacle of Travel Care service 4.0 is the all included tier. This tier incorporates the
advantages of both basic and premium tiers and goes even further by including cancellation for any reason,
providing unprecedented flexibility in customers' travel plans. Furthermore, as an additional perk featured in
the all included tier, we extend a $150 voucher for the fully unused Travel care service. The Travel Care
Service 4.0 represents a holistic approach to travel support, ensuring every aspect of a traveler's journey is
safeguarded and enhanced. Now let's go over each benefit in detail. We will start with the benefits included
in the basic tier price drop protection. Under this service, Skylux will engage in continuous monitoring of the
price for the booked ticket for a period of 24 hours post-booking. In case the price of the exact itinerary drops
below the original cost, Skylux will promptly issue a voucher to the customer, reflecting the difference in
price. The value of the price drop will be converted into a Skylux credit voucher, offering the traveler the
flexibility to use it within the upcoming year for purchasing flights, reserving hotels, or opt for any other
services offered by Skylux. This credit voucher is also transferable, offering the convenience of its use by
individuals other than the traveler in whose name the voucher was originally received. Meanwhile, it is
important to note that the maximum amount for a price drop voucher stands at $1000 and customers
cannot combine multiple vouchers for a single purchase. As well, it has no cash value, meaning that
customers cannot request the price drop amount to be refunded to their credit card. In some cases, there
might be a few price drops during the 1st 24 hours after the ticket issuance. In such a case, only one
voucher will be issued, the one for the highest price drop amount. The subsequent advantage outlined within
the TCS basic tier is flight delay and cancellation coverage. This service provides the customer with a
complimentary rebooking facility in instances where the originally scheduled flight faces disruption on the
day of travel. Consider the potential confusion experienced by our esteemed customers when finding
themselves already at the airport, fully prepared for their journey, only to receive notice of flight delay or
cancellation. This situation is further aggravated if the customers adhere to the exact timetable,
necessitating their presence at the destination at a precise time. Additionally, our commitment extends to
encompass two supplementary benefits as part of this coverage, namely the missed connections guarantee
and the service satisfaction guarantee. In order to be eligible of this service, the customer should meet the
following conditions. The delay or cancellation must occur while being in transit and traveling to or from
destination. In transit means the disruption must have occurred on the day of the original scheduled flight.
As well, the delay or cancellation must be due to an eligible reason outside of passengers' control. Eligible
reasons include, but are not limited to delays due to aircraft issues or weather. One of the most important
regulations here says that the service will be available only if, on the date of the scheduled departure, the
airline does not offer a reasonable rebooking option. A reasonable rebooking option is a new flight that either
departs within one hour of the original scheduled departure time, if available or departs on the next available
flight. If the delay or cancellation happens late in the day and there are no suitable departing flights, the
next available flight may be on the following day. The determination of whether an airline's rebooking option
is reasonable will be made at Skylux's discretion. Please remember that the maximum rebooking covered
cost is $1000 per passenger. If rebooking costs exceed the service cap of $1000 customers will be required
to pay the excess. Consider the scenario where a customer contacts us while situated at the airport,
informing us of the airline's announcement regarding the cancellation of their flight. Our VIP support
representative investigates the situation and determines that the airline has not offered a reasonable
rebooking option. Consequently, we initiate the rebooking process. Upon verifying, we see that there is
another flight departing in 1 hour, and the total rebooking cost, including fare difference, is $700. In this
case, we will rebook the ticket with zero expenses from the client's side. If in the same situation, the
rebooking cost per passenger is $1200 then we will cover $1000 while the customer will have to pay only the
remaining $200. As we already mentioned, flight delay and cancellation coverage also includes a sub-benefit
named 100% service satisfaction guarantee. If our traveler is eligible for and elect to use the benefit of flight
delay and cancellation, and he is not happy with the rebooking options offered by Skylux, we will refund back
up to $500 per passenger as a Skylux credit voucher. The voucher is valid for 1 year from the issue date and
is a temporary incentive that has no cash value. The voucher may be used only on purchases of flights, hotel
stays, or car rentals via Skylux exclusively. The main condition for this benefit validity is that the arrival to
the final destination should be delayed by 6 hours or more compared to the original arrival time. Customers
are not eligible to receive this 100% service satisfaction guarantee refund in the event the airline refunds
them the cost of the missed or canceled flight in cash or future travel credits. Please note that in cases when
the initial flight departed is scheduled, however, the client missed the connecting flight due to personal
reasons, like shopping, the benefit will be invalid. Moving on to the premium tier, the first additional benefit
the customer gets while activating premium tier is change for any reason. It is a valuable aspect of our
services designed to provide flexibility for travelers. Here's how it works. When the customer activates the
premium plan, they get the chance to change their plans once. If they decide to make changes before the
first scheduled departure, we can adjust the entire itinerary following the rules in the service terms. If the
customer requests the changes after completing a part of the trip, we can only adjust the remaining parts of
the ticket. It is important to mention here that changes can only be made to the date, time, and one way or
round trip nature of the booking. As well, the new itinerary must depart within 1 year from the originally
purchased flight departure time. When a traveler will use the service for changes, we'll waive the cost of the
change, up to 100% of the original ticket cost, including airline penalties and agency fees. Any extra fees,
like those for baggage or choosing seats when booking, won't be covered. If the change costs more than the
original ticket, the customer will have to cover the difference. The main idea, which you have to remember is
that in case of voluntary exchange with the premium tier, we cover agency fees and the airline penalty if it
applies. The fare difference is covered by the customer, or in case the original ticket is non-exchangeable
and nonrefundable, we book a new ticket for a client and cover up to 100% of the original ticket cost. In case
the new ticket is more expensive, client will need to cover the difference. Let us examine three distinct
scenarios to illustrate this concept. In the first scenario, assume that the ticket is changeable by the fair
rules. The initial ticket was priced at $3000. Upon review, our VIP support representative identifies an airline
penalty of $200. The price for the ticket on the new dates is also $3000. In this instance, we will cover the
$200 airline penalty, and the customer is not required to make any supplementary payments. This is
because the new ticket costs the same as the original one, so there is no fare difference. In the second
scenario, we encounter a situation where the original ticket was valued at $3000 accompanied by an airline
penalty for the exchange of $200 and the price of a ticket for new requested dates is $3100. It means that
the new ticket is $100 more than the original ticket cost. In this case, we will cover the $200 airline penalty,
and the client will have to pay $100 as this is the fare difference. In the last scenario, we would like to
specifically highlight the cases when the original ticket is nonrefundable or non-exchangeable. Answering the
main question you might have on this matter. Yes, even if the ticket is non-exchangeable or nonrefundable,
we will still charge only for the fare difference while covering the 100% of the original ticket cost. The
subsequent advantage featured within the premium tier is the all agency fees waived benefit, and as
previously mentioned, it is applicable for voluntary refund cases. Under this provision, Skylux processing
fees are exempted for passengers who are unable to embark on their scheduled journey due to any given
reason. This benefit encompasses two specific conditions. Firstly, the passenger is required to initiate the
cancellation of their reservation prior to the scheduled departure of either outbound or return flights.
Secondly, it's important to note that while our fees are waived, there is a possibility that airline imposed
penalties may still be applicable. It's essential to underline that the agency fees are waived for the first
change of the protected reservation only and cannot be used multiple times. Travelers cannot use these
benefits to cancel a booking and receive a full or partial refund, as it is applicable exclusively for voluntary
exchange cases. However, our next benefit has been specifically designed for voluntary refund cases.
Finally, let's go over the benefits, rules and conditions for the all included tier. For customers opting to
engage the Travel Care service 4.0 with the all included plan, the benefits at their disposal will be all the
benefits included in basic and premium tier, along with cancel for any reasoned benefit and $150 voucher for
future bookings with us. Let's begin with a cancel for any reason benefit. Its essence aligns precisely with its
name. A traveler holds the prerogative to cancel their flight for any reason prior to the initial scheduled
departure. With this benefit, customers can choose between either receiving 100% of the ticket cost as a
credit with the airline, which they can use for future bookings, or getting 75% of the ticket cost back as a
monetary refund. This particular benefit serves as the concluding piece in our comprehensive travel care
service offering. Encompassing all potential scenarios, it addresses involuntary delays and cancellations in
the basic tier, voluntary exchanges in the premium tier, and ultimately voluntary cancellations within the all
included tier. By activating the TCS 4.0 all included plan, our customers can be assured that their travel
experience remains uninterrupted, allowing them to wholeheartedly immerse themselves in their journey.
This benefit is valid within the following conditions. The amount the traveler gets back excludes the amount
paid for the Travel care service. This amount is nonrefundable. Traveler must cancel the flight no later than
24 hours before the first scheduled departure. All cancellations must be done through Skylux, not the airline. We will not require any reasons for the cancellation. Skylux will waive any cancellation fees for 100% credit
option. No additional expenses other than the cost of the ticket purchased through Skylux will be reimbursed.
For example, separately purchased trip insurance, hotels, or lost baggage protection. Refunds in cash option
will be processed within 10 days. 75% cash option is only applicable for fully unused tickets. Reimbursement
in the form of airline credit will be available within 24 hours of cancellation. Airline credit means that the
future booking should be done using the same airline as the one in original booking. Airline credit is valid for
one year from the issue date of the original air tickets. It cannot be combined with other vouchers or
transferred to another person, and the last benefit is a $150 voucher as a Skylux credit for future bookings
with us. The conditions here are very simple. In case if the customer activated travel care service, and luckily
their journey went smoothly without the necessity of using any of the TCS benefits, travelers are entitled for
a $150 voucher. In order to claim the voucher, travelers should send a request to the displayed email upon
trip completion. The voucher is valid for one year and can be transferred to another person. Customers can
apply for a Skylux credit voucher for any future bookings with us. However, they cannot use multiple
vouchers for the same booking. This particular benefit ensures that customers who opt for trip security not
only gain peace of mind, but are also rewarded with a partial amount that can be subsequently utilized,
thereby enhancing their overall booking experience. In the next presentation, we will go over the pricing of
